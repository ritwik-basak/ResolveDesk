# CartFlow — Return Policy

## Overview

At CartFlow, we want you to be completely satisfied with every purchase. If you are not happy with a product for any reason, our return policy makes it easy to send it back and get a resolution — whether that is a replacement, exchange, or refund.

---

## Return Window

| Category | Return Window |
|---|---|
| Electronics (phones, laptops, tablets) | 7 days from delivery |
| Clothing and footwear | 15 days from delivery |
| Home and kitchen appliances | 10 days from delivery |
| Books and stationery | 7 days from delivery |
| Beauty and personal care | 7 days (unopened only) |
| Groceries and perishables | Non-returnable |
| Customised / personalised items | Non-returnable |
| Digital downloads and software | Non-returnable |

The return window starts from the **date of delivery**, not the date of purchase.

---

## Valid Reasons for Return

You can raise a return request for the following reasons:

- **Defective or damaged product** — item arrived broken, scratched, or not functioning
- **Wrong item delivered** — you received a different product than what you ordered
- **Missing items** — part of your order was not included in the package
- **Not as described** — product significantly differs from the description or images on CartFlow
- **Size or fit issue** — applicable for clothing, footwear, and accessories only
- **Change of mind** — applicable for eligible categories within the return window

---

## Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned under any circumstances:
- Perishable goods (food, beverages)
- Undergarments, innerwear, and swimwear (hygiene reasons)
- Opened beauty, skincare, or cosmetics products
- Digital content, software licenses, and prepaid cards
- Customised products with personalisation (name engraving, custom printing)
- Hazardous materials

---

## Condition of Return

Products must be returned in the **original condition**:
- Original packaging intact (box, tags, accessories, manuals)
- For electronics: IMEI sticker, warranty card, and all accessories included
- For clothing: tags attached, unwashed, unworn
- Proof of purchase (CartFlow order ID) must be provided

Items returned in used, damaged, or incomplete condition may be rejected and sent back to you without a refund.

---

## How to Raise a Return Request

1. Log in to CartFlow and go to **My Orders**
2. Select the order and click **Return / Exchange**
3. Choose your reason from the dropdown
4. Upload a photo of the product (required for defective/wrong item claims)
5. Select your preferred resolution: Replacement, Exchange, or Refund
6. Submit — you will receive a confirmation within 24 hours

Once approved, a pickup will be scheduled within **2 business days**. The delivery agent will collect the item from your doorstep. No need to visit any courier office.

---

## Return Pickup

CartFlow arranges free reverse pickup for all valid return requests. You do not pay any return shipping charges. The pickup agent will hand you a return receipt — keep this for your records.

If your pin code is not serviceable for reverse pickup, you may be asked to self-ship the item to our nearest warehouse. In that case, CartFlow will reimburse your shipping cost (up to ₹150) after the return is verified.

---

## Return Verification

After your item is received at our warehouse, our quality team verifies it within **2 business days**. Once verified:
- If approved: refund or replacement is processed
- If rejected: the item is shipped back to you with a detailed reason

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I return part of a multi-item order?**
Yes. You can raise separate return requests for individual items within the same order.

**What if my return pickup fails?**
The carrier will attempt pickup twice. If both fail, the return request is cancelled. You can raise a new request.

**Can I exchange for a different size or colour?**
Yes, for clothing and footwear. Select "Exchange" when raising your return request and choose your preferred size or colour.
