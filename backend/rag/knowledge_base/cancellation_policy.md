# CartFlow — Cancellation Policy

## Overview

You can cancel an order on CartFlow at any time before it is dispatched from our warehouse. Once dispatched, cancellation is no longer possible — but you can refuse delivery or initiate a return after it arrives.

---

## How to Cancel an Order

1. Go to **My Orders** in the CartFlow app or website
2. Select the order you want to cancel
3. Click **Cancel Order**
4. Choose a cancellation reason from the dropdown
5. Confirm — the cancellation is immediate if the order has not been dispatched

You will receive an SMS and email confirmation of the cancellation within a few minutes.

---

## Cancellation Window

| Order Status | Can Cancel? |
|---|---|
| Order Placed (Payment Pending) | Yes — immediately |
| Order Confirmed (Payment Received) | Yes — up to dispatch |
| Preparing for Dispatch | Yes — but may not process before dispatch |
| Dispatched / Shipped | No — refuse delivery or return after receipt |
| Out for Delivery | No — refuse delivery at doorstep |
| Delivered | No — initiate return within return window |

**"Preparing for Dispatch"** is a brief window where we cannot guarantee cancellation. If your cancellation request is received but the order is already dispatched, a full refund is still issued after the item is returned to our warehouse.

---

## Refund After Cancellation

| Payment Method | Refund Timeline After Cancellation |
|---|---|
| UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm UPI) | 1–3 business days |
| Debit card | 3–5 business days |
| Credit card | 5–7 business days |
| CartFlow Wallet | Instant |
| Cash on Delivery | No charge collected — nothing to refund |

For COD orders cancelled before delivery, no charge is collected. For COD orders where you refused delivery, the return shipping is handled by CartFlow at no cost to you.

---

## Cancelling Part of an Order

If your order contains multiple items, you can cancel individual items:
1. Go to **My Orders > Select Order**
2. Click **Cancel** next to the specific item (not the whole order)
3. The remaining items will continue to be processed and delivered

Refund is calculated proportionally based on the cancelled item's price.

---

## Can a Seller Cancel My Order?

In rare cases, a seller may cancel an order if:
- The product goes out of stock after your order was placed
- A pricing error was detected
- The item cannot be dispatched to your pin code

If a seller cancels your order, you will receive:
- An immediate notification with the reason
- A full refund to your original payment method
- An option to find an alternative product

CartFlow takes seller cancellations seriously. Sellers with high cancellation rates are penalised and may be removed from the platform.

---

## What if I Want to Change My Order Instead of Cancelling?

CartFlow does not support order modifications after placement. If you want to:
- **Change colour/size** → Cancel the current order and place a new one
- **Add items** → Place a separate new order
- **Change address** → Contact support immediately (only possible before dispatch)
- **Change payment method** → Not possible after payment is completed

---

## Cancellation During Sale Events

During major sale events (Big Billion Days, Great Indian Festival), order processing is accelerated. Your order may move to "Dispatched" status faster than usual, reducing the cancellation window. We recommend reviewing your cart carefully before placing an order during sales.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**I tried to cancel but the button is greyed out. Why?**
This means your order has already been dispatched. You can refuse the delivery when the agent arrives, or return it after delivery.

**I cancelled but haven't received my refund yet.**
Check **My Orders > View Refund Status**. The refund timeline starts from the cancellation confirmation, not the date you initiated it. UPI refunds typically arrive within 1–3 business days.

**My order is showing "Cancelled" but I did not cancel it.**
This could be a seller cancellation (stock issue or pricing error). Check your email for a cancellation notification from CartFlow. A full refund will be processed automatically.

**Can I cancel after refusing delivery?**
If you refused delivery, the package is returned to our warehouse. Once it arrives, your refund is processed automatically — no further action needed from your side.
