# CartFlow — Contact Support

## Overview

CartFlow's customer support team is available to help you with orders, returns, refunds, payments, account issues, and any other concerns. We offer multiple ways to reach us — choose whichever is most convenient.

---

## Support Channels

### 1. Live Chat (Fastest)
Available 24/7 in the CartFlow app and website.

- Open the CartFlow app → tap the **Help** icon (bottom navigation)
- Or go to cartflow.in → click **Help & Support** → **Chat with Us**
- Average wait time: under 2 minutes
- Best for: order tracking, return requests, payment issues

### 2. Phone Support
**Toll-Free: 1800-XXX-XXXX**
Available: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM IST
Sunday: 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST

- Best for: urgent issues, complex problems requiring detailed explanation
- Language support: Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati

### 3. Email Support
**support@cartflow.in**
Response time: Within 24 hours (business days)
Best for: complaints requiring documentation, legal queries, account deletion requests

### 4. CartFlow Help Centre
Visit: help.cartflow.in
- Browse 500+ self-help articles
- Searchable by topic (returns, payments, shipping, etc.)
- No login required

## Self-Service (Resolve Without Calling)

Many issues can be resolved instantly through **My Orders** without contacting support:

| Issue | Self-Service Action |
|---|---|
| Track my order | My Orders → Track Shipment |
| Cancel my order | My Orders → Cancel Order |
| Raise a return | My Orders → Return / Exchange |
| Check refund status | My Orders → View Refund Status |
| Download invoice | My Orders → View Invoice |
| Report wrong item | My Orders → Report a Problem |
| Update delivery address | My Orders → Edit Address (before dispatch only) |

---

## Escalation — When to Ask for a Senior Agent

If your issue is not resolved satisfactorily, you can escalate:

**In chat**: Type "Escalate to supervisor" or click the **Escalate** button in the chat window
**By phone**: Say "I want to speak to a senior agent" — the agent will transfer you
**By email**: Reply to any previous support email with "ESCALATION REQUEST" in the subject line

Escalations are prioritised and typically resolved within 24–48 hours.

## Tips for Faster Resolution

1. **Have your Order ID ready** — It's in your confirmation SMS/email (e.g., ORD-10001)
2. **Check My Orders first** — Many issues show their status there without needing to contact us
3. **Be specific** — "My order ORD-10001 shows delivered but I haven't received it" is faster to resolve than "my order is missing"
4. **Keep photos handy** — For wrong or damaged item claims, attach a photo from the start
5. **Note your case/ticket ID** — Every support interaction generates a ticket ID; use it for follow-ups

---

## Support During Sale Events

During high-traffic sale events (Big Billion Days, Great Indian Festival, Diwali Sale), response times may be slightly longer:
- Live chat: up to 10 minutes wait
- Phone: up to 20 minutes wait
- Email: up to 48 hours

We recommend using the **self-service options** in My Orders during peak periods for the fastest resolution.
