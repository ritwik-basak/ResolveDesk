# CartFlow — Product FAQ

## How do I search for a product on CartFlow?

You can find products in several ways:
- **Search bar** — Type the product name, brand, or keyword at the top of the app or website
- **Category browse** — Tap a category (Electronics, Fashion, Home & Kitchen, etc.) from the homepage
- **Voice search** — Tap the microphone icon in the search bar and speak your query
- **Scan & Search** — Use the camera icon to scan a product barcode to find it on CartFlow

Use **filters** to narrow results by price, brand, rating, discount, delivery speed, and more.

---

## How do I know if a product is genuine?

CartFlow has a strict seller verification process. Signs of a genuine product:
- **CartFlow Assured** badge — Products sold directly by CartFlow or its verified warehouse partners; all items quality-checked before dispatch
- **Brand Authorised Seller** tag — Seller is an official distributor of that brand
- **Manufacturer Warranty** label — Products covered by the brand's Indian warranty

For electronics (phones, laptops), look for:
- ISI or BIS certification mark (for Indian safety standards)
- MRP label in INR
- India warranty card included in the box

If you receive a product you suspect is counterfeit, report it immediately via **My Orders > Report a Problem**. CartFlow takes counterfeiting seriously and will investigate.

---

## What does "CartFlow Assured" mean?

**CartFlow Assured** products are:
- Stored in CartFlow's own fulfilment centres (not third-party seller warehouses)
- Quality-checked before dispatch
- Eligible for faster delivery (often next-day or 2-day)
- Covered by CartFlow's return policy directly (no dependency on third-party seller)

Assured products are the most reliable choice, especially for high-value purchases.

## Can I check product availability at my pin code before ordering?

Yes. On any product page:
1. Enter your PIN code in the **Check Delivery** box
2. CartFlow will show whether the product is deliverable to your location
3. You will also see the estimated delivery date and available delivery options (standard, express, same-day)

If a product is not deliverable to your pin code, you can click **Notify Me** to receive an alert when it becomes available.

---

## How do I read product ratings and reviews?

Every product on CartFlow has a rating (out of 5) and customer reviews. To read them:
- Scroll down on the product page to the **Ratings & Reviews** section
- Filter reviews by star rating, with or without photos, verified purchases only
- Look for the **Verified Purchase** badge — this confirms the reviewer actually bought the product on CartFlow

CartFlow does not allow sellers to delete or modify reviews. Reviews from fake or incentivised purchases are removed by our moderation system.

## The product I want is out of stock. What can I do?

If a product shows **Out of Stock**:
1. Click **Notify Me** — you will receive a push notification + SMS when it is back in stock
2. Check similar products — CartFlow shows alternatives from other sellers below the out-of-stock product
3. Check back during sale events — many brands restock specifically for CartFlow sales (Big Billion Days, Great Indian Festival, etc.)

---

## What is the difference between different sellers for the same product?

Multiple sellers may list the same product on CartFlow at different prices. Key differences:
- **Price** — Sellers set their own prices; compare them before buying
- **Delivery time** — Warehouse location affects shipping speed; CartFlow Assured is typically fastest
- **Seller rating** — Shown as a percentage; higher is better
- **Return policy** — Some sellers have stricter conditions; check the product page for seller-specific policy

Clicking **See all sellers** on a product page shows all available options side by side.

---

## How do I compare products before buying?

On the CartFlow app and website:
1. Long-press (app) or hover (website) on a product card
2. Select **Add to Compare**
3. Add up to 4 products in the same category
4. Click **Compare Now** — a side-by-side table of specifications, prices, ratings, and delivery times is shown

---

## Does CartFlow sell second-hand or refurbished products?

Yes. CartFlow has a separate **Refurbished** section under Electronics. All refurbished products:
- Are graded (Grade A = near-new, Grade B = minor cosmetic marks, Grade C = visible wear)
- Come with a **6-month CartFlow warranty**
- Are tested for full functionality before listing
- Are priced significantly lower than new equivalents

Refurbished products have a 7-day return window for any functional issues.

---

## I received a product different from what was shown online. What do I do?

Raise a **Wrong Item Delivered** return request immediately:
1. Go to **My Orders > Select Order > Return / Exchange**
2. Choose **Wrong Item Delivered** as the reason
3. Upload a clear photo of the product you received
4. Submit — CartFlow will approve the return within 24 hours and schedule a free pickup

CartFlow will send you the correct product as a replacement, or issue a full refund if the item is no longer available.
