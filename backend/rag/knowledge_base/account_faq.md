# CartFlow — Account FAQ

## How do I create a CartFlow account?

You can sign up on CartFlow in two ways:

**Mobile number (recommended):**
1. Go to cartflow.in or open the CartFlow app
2. Click **Sign Up**
3. Enter your mobile number — an OTP is sent via SMS
4. Verify the OTP and set your name and email address
5. Your account is ready

**Google or Apple sign-in:**
- Click **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple** — no password needed
- Your Google/Apple account details are used to create your CartFlow profile

---

## How do I reset my password?

1. Go to the CartFlow login page
2. Click **Forgot Password**
3. Enter your registered email address or mobile number
4. You will receive a reset link via email or OTP via SMS
5. Follow the link / enter OTP to set a new password

If you signed up via Google or Apple, you do not have a CartFlow password — use the same Google/Apple sign-in method.

---

## How do I update my phone number or email?

**To change phone number:**
1. Go to **My Account > Profile > Edit**
2. Click **Change Mobile Number**
3. Enter the new number — OTP verification required for both old and new numbers

**To change email:**
1. Go to **My Account > Profile > Edit**
2. Click **Change Email**
3. Enter the new email address
4. A verification link is sent to the new email — click it to confirm

Note: You cannot change the phone number or email to one already registered with another CartFlow account.

---

## How do I add or manage saved addresses?

1. Go to **My Account > Saved Addresses**
2. Click **Add New Address**
3. Fill in: full name, flat/house/building, street/area, city, state, and PIN code
4. Mark one address as **Default** — it will be pre-selected at every checkout

You can save up to **5 addresses** on your account. To edit or delete an address, click the three-dot menu next to it.

## What is the CartFlow Loyalty Programme?

CartFlow Rewards is our loyalty programme:
- Earn **1 point for every ₹10 spent**
- Points credited within 24 hours of order delivery
- Redeem points at checkout: **100 points = ₹10 discount**
- Points expire after 12 months of inactivity

You can check your point balance in **My Account > CartFlow Rewards**.

## How do I view my order history?

Go to **My Account > My Orders**. All orders — current, delivered, cancelled, and returned — are listed here with:
- Order ID, date, and amount
- Current status and tracking link
- Option to raise return/exchange requests
- Invoice download link

---

## How do I delete my CartFlow account?

Account deletion is permanent. All your order history, saved addresses, and CartFlow Wallet balance will be erased.

To delete your account:
1. Go to **My Account > Settings > Privacy**
2. Click **Delete My Account**
3. Read the consequences and confirm with your password or OTP
4. Your account enters a 30-day deactivation period — you can reactivate by logging in
5. After 30 days, your data is permanently deleted per our Privacy Policy

If you have an active order, open return, or wallet balance, you cannot delete the account until those are resolved.

---

## I cannot log in to my account. What should I do?

Common causes and fixes:
- **Wrong password** → Use **Forgot Password** to reset
- **OTP not received** → Check network signal; try resending after 60 seconds; check if the number is registered with CartFlow
- **Account temporarily locked** → After 5 failed login attempts, the account is locked for 30 minutes for security
- **Number not registered** → You may have signed up with a different number or email — try the other
- **Google/Apple sign-in** → If you used social login, use the same method instead of password login

For persistent issues, contact CartFlow support with your registered email address or phone number.

---

## How do I add family members to my account?

CartFlow supports **Family Sharing**:
1. Go to **My Account > Family Sharing**
2. Invite up to 4 family members via their mobile number
3. Each member has their own login but orders are grouped under the primary account
4. The primary account holder can see all family orders and manage addresses

Family members do not share wallet balance or rewards points — each has their own.

---

## Is my personal data safe with CartFlow?

CartFlow follows all guidelines under India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act)**. Your data is:
- Never sold to third parties
- Used only for order fulfilment, personalisation, and communication
- Stored on servers in India
- Accessible by you at any time via **My Account > Download My Data**

For privacy-related queries or data deletion requests, email privacy@cartflow.in.
