# CartFlow — Shipping Policy

## Overview

CartFlow ships to all major cities, towns, and pin codes across India. We partner with trusted logistics providers including Delhivery, BlueDart, Ekart Logistics, Ecom Express, and India Post to ensure your orders reach you safely and on time.

---

## Shipping Charges

| Order Value | Shipping Charge |
|---|---|
| Below ₹499 | ₹49 flat fee |
| ₹499 and above | FREE standard delivery |
| Express delivery (any order) | ₹99 additional |
| Same-day delivery (select cities) | ₹149 additional |

Same-day delivery is currently available in Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, and Ahmedabad for orders placed before 12:00 PM IST.

---

## Estimated Delivery Timelines

| Delivery Type | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, etc.) | 1–3 business days |
| Tier-2 cities (Jaipur, Lucknow, Indore, etc.) | 3–5 business days |
| Tier-3 cities and remote towns | 5–8 business days |
| Rural and remote pin codes | 7–12 business days |
| Express delivery | Next business day (metro only) |

Business days are Monday to Saturday, excluding national public holidays. Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays are processed the next working day.

---

## Order Processing Time

All orders are verified and dispatched within **1 business day** of payment confirmation. During sale events (Big Billion Days, festive sales), processing may take up to **2 business days** due to high order volumes.

You will receive an SMS and email confirmation with your tracking number once your order is dispatched. Tracking is available through the CartFlow app, website, or directly on the carrier's tracking portal.

---

## Tracking Your Order

To track your shipment:
1. Log in to your CartFlow account
2. Go to **My Orders**
3. Click on the order you want to track
4. Click **Track Shipment** — this shows real-time carrier updates

You can also enter your tracking number directly on Delhivery's website (delhivery.com), BlueDart's portal (bluedart.com), or the respective carrier's site.

---

## Multiple Items in One Order

If you order multiple items, they may be shipped in separate packages from different warehouses. Each package will have its own tracking number. You will receive a separate tracking update for each package. All items in your order are covered under the same delivery timeline promise.

---

## Shipping to Remote or Difficult Areas

Certain pin codes in hilly regions (Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Northeastern states) and islands (Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep) may have extended delivery timelines of 10–15 business days. Shipping charges for these areas may differ and will be displayed clearly at checkout before you place your order.

---

## Failed Delivery Attempts

If the delivery agent is unable to reach you:
- A delivery attempt notification is sent via SMS
- The carrier makes up to **3 delivery attempts**
- After 3 failed attempts, the package is returned to our warehouse
- You will receive a full refund (including original shipping charges) within 5–7 business days

To avoid failed deliveries, ensure your address and phone number are correct before placing your order.

---

## Address Changes After Placing an Order

Address changes can only be requested **before the order is dispatched**. Once dispatched, we cannot redirect the shipment. To change your address, contact CartFlow support immediately via chat or call 1800-XXX-XXXX (toll-free).

---

## Damaged in Transit

If your package appears tampered with or damaged upon arrival, do not accept the delivery. Raise a complaint immediately through the CartFlow app or call our support line. CartFlow will arrange a replacement or full refund for items damaged in transit at no cost to you.
