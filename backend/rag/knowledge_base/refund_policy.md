# CartFlow — Refund Policy

## Overview

Once CartFlow approves your return or cancellation, we process your refund as quickly as possible. The refund goes back to your original payment method — UPI, bank account, credit/debit card, or CartFlow Wallet — depending on how you paid.

---

## Refund Timeline

| Payment Method | Refund Timeline |
|---|---|
| UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm UPI) | 1–3 business days |
| Debit card (Rupay, Visa, Mastercard) | 3–5 business days |
| Credit card (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, etc.) | 5–7 business days |
| Net banking | 3–5 business days |
| Paytm Wallet / Amazon Pay | 1–2 business days |
| CartFlow Wallet | Instant (within minutes) |
| Cash on Delivery (COD) | 5–7 business days to bank account |

These timelines begin **after CartFlow confirms your refund** — not from the day you raise the return request. Allow additional time for bank processing.

---

## Refund for Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders

For COD orders, CartFlow cannot refund cash to your doorstep. Instead, refunds are processed as:
1. **CartFlow Wallet** (instant) — use for future purchases on CartFlow
2. **Bank transfer (NEFT)** — you will be asked to provide your bank account number and IFSC code during the return process; takes 5–7 business days

---

## Partial Refunds

In some cases you may receive a partial refund:
- If only part of your order is returned
- If the product is returned with minor damage or missing accessories (deduction applied)
- If a promotional discount was applied at the time of purchase and only part of the order is returned (discount is recomputed)

---

## Refund Status — How to Check

1. Go to **My Orders** in the CartFlow app or website
2. Click on the relevant order
3. Click **View Refund Status**

You will see the current stage: Approved → Initiated → Processed. You will also receive SMS and email updates at each stage.

Alternatively, check your bank's transaction history or UPI app — the refund will appear as a credit from "CartFlow Payments."

---

## Refund Not Received

If you have not received your refund within the expected timeline:

1. Check your bank statement or UPI transaction history first
2. Confirm the refund was approved in **My Orders > View Refund Status**
3. If status shows "Processed" but amount not received, contact your bank
4. If status shows "Initiated" but timeline has passed, contact CartFlow support with your order ID

Common reasons for delay:
- Bank processing delays (weekends and public holidays add time)
- Incorrect bank account details provided for COD refunds
- Credit card company processing time (independent of CartFlow)

---

## Refund on Cancelled Orders

- **Before dispatch**: Full refund within 1–3 business days (UPI/cards)
- **After dispatch but before delivery**: Full refund processed after the item is returned to our warehouse (7–10 business days total)
- **After delivery**: Standard return process applies; refund after return verification

---

## Failed Refund Transactions

If a refund fails (e.g., your bank account was closed, UPI ID changed), CartFlow will contact you via email and SMS within 48 hours to collect alternate payment details. Failing to respond within 7 days may result in the refund being converted to CartFlow Wallet credit.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I get my refund in CartFlow Wallet instead of my original payment method?**
Yes. During the return process, select "Refund to CartFlow Wallet" for instant credit. Wallet credits never expire and can be used on any future CartFlow purchase.

**Will I get a refund if my order is lost in transit?**
Yes. If your order is confirmed lost by the carrier, CartFlow will issue a full refund or send a replacement — your choice — within 5 business days of confirming the loss.

**Is shipping charge refunded?**
Original shipping charge is refunded only if the return is due to CartFlow's fault (wrong item, defective product). For change-of-mind returns, shipping charges are non-refundable.
